This Privacy Policy explains what information Sear ("Sear", "we", "us") handles when you use the Sear app for iPhone, why it is needed, where it is kept, and how you can delete it. By using Sear you agree to this policy. Please also read our Terms of Use.

1. What Sear does

Sear is a personal skin diary. Each day you can take one photo, rate how your skin looks on a 1–5 scale, tap where spots appear on a face map, tag what happened that day (sleep, stress, diet, period), list the products you used, and add a short note or voice note. Sear then shows your timeline and describes patterns in what you logged.

Sear does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Patterns are simple statistics from your own entries.

4. Facial data

This chapter is Sear's complete explanation of how the app collects, uses, discloses, shares and retains facial data.

4.1 What facial data Sear collects

Sear does not collect biometric identifiers. It does not create face geometry, faceprints, face templates or any face-recognition data, and it does not use Face ID, ARKit face tracking or the TrueDepth camera for face mapping.

The only face-related information is:

  • photos you choose to take or pick for your entries, which may show part or all of your face; and
  • spots you tap by hand on a generic face diagram (for example "chin" or "left cheek"). These are positions on a drawing, not measurements of your face.

4.2 How facial data is used

Your photos and face-map spots are used only to show your own entries back to you: in your timeline, in the entry details, as a faint overlay that helps you line up the next photo, in comparison cards, and in the PDF report you create. Severity charts and patterns are calculated from the ratings, spots and tags you entered, not from analyzing the image. Sear does not run facial analysis, skin analysis or any AI model on your photos.

4.3 Disclosure and sharing

Sear does not share, sell, rent or disclose your photos or face-map data to us or to any third party, and does not send them to any third-party service. Photos only leave the app when you choose to export them yourself, for example by saving a comparison card to Photos or sharing a PDF report or backup file through the iOS share sheet. Where they go after that is controlled by you and the app you share to.

4.4 Where facial data is stored and how long it is kept

Photos and face-map spots are stored in Sear's private app storage on your iPhone. They are kept until you delete the entry, use Delete All Data, or delete the app. iOS may include app data in your own device backups according to your iCloud or computer backup settings.

4.5 How to delete facial data

  • Delete a single entry (and its photo) from the entry details screen.
  • Go to Settings → Delete All Data to remove every entry, photo and voice note at once.
  • Deleting the Sear app also removes all of its data from your iPhone.

5. In-App Purchases

Sear offers optional Credits packs through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Credits can be used for extra exports, such as a comparison card without a watermark, a pattern report (PDF), or more slots for your own products. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive or store your payment card details. We keep a record of your Credits balance and of the purchase identifiers already credited, so the same purchase is not counted twice.

6. Analytics, advertising and tracking

Sear does not include advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell your personal data.

7. Exports and backups you create

You can export a comparison card, a pattern report (PDF), all photos, or a backup file (JSON). These files are created only when you ask for them. Once you save or share them, they are handled by the destination you choose. You can import a Sear backup file from the start screen with I already have a log.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • End Session (Settings) returns you to the start screen. Your entries are kept and are there again when you tap Start tracking.
  • Delete All Data (Settings) permanently removes your entries, photos, voice notes, drafts, profile, custom products and Credits balance from the app, and returns you to the start screen. This cannot be undone. Built-in content such as the sample entry and the product library stays in the app.

If you have questions about your data, contact us at SearSerch@gmail.com.

9. Children's privacy

Sear is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will help remove it.

10. Security

Your data is protected by iOS app sandboxing and by your device passcode. No method of storage is completely secure, so please keep your device protected and your iOS software up to date.
